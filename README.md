# PeacePulse

**Detect early. Verify. Respond. Prevent conflict.**

PeacePulse is a people-centred proof of concept for the **Stability & Social Cohesion** track of the OSF × Andela hackathon.

## Core flow

**Report → AI Triage → Human Verification → Alert → Mediation → Resolution**

## What it demonstrates

- Community incident reporting
- Anonymous reporting option
- AI-assisted risk triage
- Human verification before verified alerts
- Community alerts and notifications
- Mediation and resolution workflow
- Youth, parent/guardian and community safety resources
- Privacy-preserving illustrative community risk zones
- Role-based simulated access
- End-to-end synthetic demo mode

## Responsible AI

AI is used as decision support. It does not determine guilt, accuse individuals, or replace human responders or emergency services.

## Prototype limitations

- Browser localStorage is used for prototype data.
- Authentication is simulated.
- The risk map is illustrative, not a live geographic system.
- Demo incidents are synthetic.
- No real-world impact statistics are claimed.

## Production roadmap

Secure backend storage, real authentication/authorization, encryption, privacy and retention controls, reliable notifications, multilingual/low-bandwidth access, community co-design, responsible AI evaluation, monitoring and abuse prevention.

## Hackathon fit

PeacePulse aims to help people find, understand, verify and use community information through a clear, human-led response pathway.
